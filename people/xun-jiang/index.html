I am an Assistant Professor in the Spatial Intelligence Group (SIG) , Tongji University. Before that, I earned my Ph.D. in the Successive Postgraduate and Doctoral Program at the CFM lab of the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), supervised by Prof. Heng Tao Shen, co-supervised by Prof. Fumin Shen and Prof. Xing Xu. I also spent a great time in MReal Lab at Nanyang Technological University under the supervision of Prof. Hanwang Zhang.

In the past five years, I have published more than 20 papers on AI/Multimedia flagship conferences and ACM/IEEE transaction journals, including IEEE TPAMI, TIP, TMM, TNNLS, TCSVT, TFS, CVPR, ACM MM, AAAI, etc. I am also serving as a journal reviewer for IEEE TPAMI, TIP, TMM, TCSVT, ACM TOIS, TOMM, etc., as well as a Program Committee member for CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ACM MM, AAAI, etc.

I'm working on multimodal learning, spatial intelligence (particularly from an egocentric view), VLM/LLM reasoning enhancement, and am also highly interested in embodied multimodal spatial perception and navigation. Currently, I am leading an interest group under SIG, namely Multimodal Intelligence & Physical Application Lab (MIPAL). Feel free to contact me for discussion and collaboration!

We are currently recruiting Master's and Doctoral students for enrollment in 2027. If you are a diligent student who enjoys multimodal research and practical robotic applications, you are welcome to join MIPAL and grow together with us!
