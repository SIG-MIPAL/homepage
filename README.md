# MIPAL

**Multimodal Intelligence & Physical Application Lab (MIPAL)** is a research lab within the
[Spatial Intelligence Group (SIG)](https://sig.tongji.edu.cn/) at Tongji University, led by
Dr. Xun Jiang. Our research spans multimodal learning, spatial intelligence (particularly from
an egocentric view), and VLM/LLM reasoning, with a strong interest in embodied multimodal
spatial perception and navigation.
